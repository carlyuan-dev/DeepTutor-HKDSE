Task: Write an article for a school newsletter explaining whether the school should organise a monthly repair afternoon. Discuss one benefit, one limitation and a practical suggestion.
Genre: Feature Article (article)

Essay (paste the text below into Student Essay):

Give Repair a Chance

Our school should organise a monthly repair afternoon. Many students throw away pencil cases when only the zip is broken. Learning a simple repair could save money and help us think more carefully about what we buy. Working together could also allow younger students to learn from older ones.

However, repair is not always the best choice. Some objects need special equipment and an unsafe repair may hurt someone. Students are also busy, so a long session after school might exclude people who have other activities. The school should not promise that every broken item can be saved.

I suggest starting with one lunchtime demonstration and a voluntary afternoon session. Teachers could choose a few safe types of item, such as clothes and book covers. The organisers should check later whether the repaired items still work, rather than judging success only by how many people attend.

If the first sessions are useful, the school can continue them. A small, careful experiment is more convincing than a large event with no follow-up.
